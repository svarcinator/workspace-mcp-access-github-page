# Privacy policy — Andy

Andy is used only by its owner for personal use.

- Data from Google APIs (Calendar, Gmail, Contacts, Drive) is accessed only
  after the owner signs in and gives consent.
- The data is processed locally on the owner's computer and by the AI model
  provider used by the assistant. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
- OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked
  at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
